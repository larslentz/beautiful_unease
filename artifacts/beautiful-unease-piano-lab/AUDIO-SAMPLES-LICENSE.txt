Piano sample attribution
------------------------

The piano samples served from public/piano-samples are from Salamander Grand
Piano V3 by Alexander Holm.

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Bundled from:
  @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity1@1.0.5
  @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity8@1.0.5
  @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity16@1.0.5

The online app loads the required MP3 files from its own site when playback
starts. The samples are included unchanged from these packages. Each velocity
layer contains the same 30 pitch-matched notes.
